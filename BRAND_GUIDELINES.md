# MedBuddy — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence

MedBuddy is the digital bridge between families and their health — a calm, reliable companion that helps you stay on top of medications, check-ups, and health routines with empathy and ease.

**Vision**
To empower every family to manage the health of their loved ones with clarity, confidence, and care.

**Mission**
To simplify everyday healthcare through intelligent organization, proactive reminders, and tailored guidance for every stage of life.

---

## Target Audience

**Working Professionals Abroad**
- Managing care from a distance for loved ones at home.
- Secure digital records, alerts, coordination with local caregivers.

**Young Parents**
- Managing vaccinations, prescriptions, and health records for kids.
- Growth, medical and vaccination trackers, pediatric milestones, reminders.

**Adults (22–35 yrs)**
- Caring for aging parents while managing their own families.
- Medication tracking, doctor reminders, shared access with siblings and families.

---

## Primary Logo

More than just a symbol, the MedBuddy Primary Logo embodies organized care — a blend of compassion and precision.

The logo reflects empathy and love, while its clean, structured form signifies order and reliability. Together, they remind our users that managing health for loved ones can be both simple and meaningful.

## Logo Mark

The MedBuddy logo mark — two pills forming a heart — emphasizes care and precision in health management, and the design underscores empathy and reliability, connecting directly to the brand's core values.

## Logo in Monochrome

The logo is available in both:
- Green-on-black
- Black-on-white

(icon-only and full lockup with wordmark, for each variant)

## Brand Colors

Our color palette of Green and White reflects clarity, care, and trust. The vibrant green conveys health, vitality, and reassurance, creating a friendly and confident tone. The clean white represents purity and professionalism, reinforcing safety and simplicity. Together, they create a fresh and trustworthy identity suited for modern healthcare experiences.

## Typography

**Primary typeface: Poppins**

MedBuddy uses Poppins as its primary font to create a clear, approachable, and trustworthy experience. The font's clean, geometric shapes enhance readability and visual balance, making medical information easy to digest. Its modern yet friendly tone reflects MedBuddy's commitment to accessible and user-centered care. Overall, Poppins helps establish a professional and welcoming identity across all parts of the app.
